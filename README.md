## 📌 Project Overview
**Cyreena**is an Affordable Luxury fashion brand specializing in high-end, contemporary Modest Fashion.

## 🧠 Case Study Structure

### 1️⃣ The Problem
- Limited digital visibility  
- Low engagement rate  
- Inconsistent content strategy  
- Weak conversion from followers to customers  

### 2️⃣ Strategy
We built a **content-driven marketing strategy** focusing on:  
 Operates on a "Limited Edition" drop model via D2C social media channels (Facebook/Instagram).

### 3️⃣ Execution
**📸 Content Production**  
- Professional food photography  
- Behind-the-scenes kitchen shots  
- Before/After meal visuals  
- Customer experience highlights  

**🎬 Video Marketing**  
- 2–3 Reels per week  
- Offer-based short videos  
- Story-based engagement posts  

**📢 Paid Ads**  
- Engagement Campaigns  
- Conversion Campaigns  
- Geo-targeted local audience ads  

**💬 Community Management**  
- Fast response time  
- Comment engagement  
- Polls & interactive stories
